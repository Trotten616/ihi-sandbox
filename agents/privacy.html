Privacy

IntelliGro.AI · Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

Everything you put into IntelliGro.AI stays on your device. There's no account, no server of ours and no analytics. Nothing is sent anywhere except the webhook requests you set up and choose to run.

What's stored, and where

  • Your agents, groups, notes, pins, quick actions (including any auth headers you add) and your activity log are saved in your browser's local storage on this device.
  • If you set a device passcode, only a salted SHA-256 hash of it is stored, also on this device.
  • The app's own files are cached on the device so it opens offline. Locking the app clears that cache.

What's sent over the network

  • Loading the app downloads its files from the site that hosts it, like any web page.
  • When you run a quick action and tap Run on the confirm screen, your phone sends that request straight to the address you entered. We never see it.
  • Opening an agent follows the link you saved, which may open a website or another app.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads or crash reporting.

Exports

Export creates a file on your device. Auth headers are left out unless you choose to include them. Anyone who has an exported file can read what's in it.

Deleting your data

Use You → Erase everything, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Security note

Anyone who can use your unlocked phone can open this app and run its quick actions. Set a device passcode in You if you store webhooks or auth headers.
